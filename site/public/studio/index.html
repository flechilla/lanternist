    A magic lantern for your stories

    Give it one line. It puts on the show.

    Lanternist turns an idea into a narrated film. It writes the story, reads it aloud in your language, draws every scene with the same cast, and sets the moments you choose in motion.

    ↓ Go down and meet the crew

    Quill, the writer

    Every film starts as one line.

    Quill turns your idea into a story, then into a storyboard: who is in it, where it happens, and a list of shots. Each paragraph is cut on its sentences into one to three shots, the way an editor would: a wide shot, then a close one.

    The story is written in its own language. The notes for the pictures stay in English, which picture models follow best. Pick a language and Quill types it again.

    In the app, Quill is a language model.

    Echo, the voice

    The voice comes first.

    Echo records the narration before anything is drawn, so each picture stays on screen exactly as long as its words. Spanish is read a little slower than English, and the shots stretch to fit.

    Put on a record to hear the first line of Luna's story in any of ten languages.

    In the app, Echo is a voice model. These recordings were made by one.

    Dab, the painter

    Everyone looks like themselves, every time.

    Dab paints a cast sheet first, then every picture from the portraits of the characters in it. Luna keeps her grey coat and her amber eyes from the first shot to the last.

    Choose a style, and Dab paints the cast in it.

    Don't like a picture? Draw it again. The other pictures stay as they are.

    In the app, Dab is an image model.

    Nudge, the animator

    You choose which scenes move.

    Nudge moves Luna by hand, a frame at a time: twelve frames for every second of film. Try it. Each press of the shutter takes one frame, and Nudge moves her on a little.

    0 of 12 frames

    In the app, a video model moves the shots you mark, so nobody has to. The rest stay pictures with a slow camera move across them, and cost less.

    Splice, the editor

    Then it is all put together.

    Splice lays the narration over the pictures and sets each moving shot's own sound under the voice. Shots cut inside a paragraph and dissolve between paragraphs. Subtitles go on, and the whole film is levelled to −16 LUFS, easy on a phone speaker.

    −16 LUFS

    Change one scene later, and only that scene and the mix are made again. The rest comes from the cache.

    The lanternist's notes

    What do I need?
    A phone or a computer. Lanternist runs the models for you, so there's nothing to install and no graphics card or API keys to bring.
    What does a film cost?
    You buy credits. Before anything runs, Lanternist shows what the film will cost, and afterwards it charges only for the steps that ran.
    Can I change things?
    Every line of the script, every picture, which shots move, the narrator's voice and the style. Changing one scene makes only that scene again.
    Which languages?
    Stories are written and narrated in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean.
